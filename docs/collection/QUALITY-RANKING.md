# Finished collection — proposed quality ranking

**Historical baseline: 34 masters reviewed before the quality redesign. Ranks 23–34 have since been redesigned at the user’s request. All 34 remain; new versions await review. [Compare the twelve](../../concepts/century/quality-redesign/index.html).**

The ten newly proposed concepts are queued ideas, not finished wallpapers, and are excluded. The original fourteen use current development masters, not historical reference previews.

Comparative editorial judgement, not a scientific measurement. Ranking prioritises ultrawide masters; Century compact layouts were reviewed in the preceding refinement. Current compact parity of the original fourteen has not been re-audited in this ranking.

## Standard

A sheet must work as a silhouette, as a readable mechanism, and as a document with something specific to discover. Parts count is not a score. All five criteria matter; arbitrary data presented as real or an unconvincing central mechanism can block a sheet regardless of its beauty.

## Proposed boundary

Through rank 22, form, mechanism and story reinforce each other without a central visual redesign. Below it, the main subject needs new form, composition or a corrected explanatory panel. Added parts and text alone no longer close the gap.

Queue Garden is the closest call. Exact order within a tier is less important than the boundary.

## Approval scope

Approving the proposal would designate 22 sheets as the main release selection and 12 as a rework queue. It would not delete source files, revive the 80 rejected Century concepts, or automatically install/publish anything.
