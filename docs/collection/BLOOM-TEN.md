# p(bloom) — ten sincere machines

Brief for a new series of ten wallpapers (series id `b01`–`b10`). The first 42
sheets often lean on irony (Sock Oracle, Advice Filter, Compliment Mill). These
ten must not. They are sincere optimism: a future in which something important
went right, documented calmly and precisely. No jokes, no winks, no satire, no
pastiche of corporate copy. The humour budget is zero; the warmth comes from
what each machine gives to specific people.

Every machine rests on real, current science or engineering and one honest
breakthrough that is still missing. Numbers are plausible and internally
consistent; they are narrative, not engineering claims. Human presence is
implied by scale, handles, seats, labels and field notes — faces are not needed.

Tone of the texts: plain, specific, generous. Say what it does, for whom, and
what changes in someone's day. Avoid grand words (revolutionary, miracle,
utopia). Prefer "a nine-year-old in Kisumu books Saturn for Tuesday night" over
"democratizing astronomy".

## Production

Same standard as the Century series: original Blender model built with
`tools/century/kit.py`, orthographic views exported as visible vector paths,
Cairo composition with shared typography, A/B/C views and field notes. Follow
`docs/WALLPAPER-PRODUCTION-GUIDE.pl.md` (silhouette first, purposeful detail,
empty space is part of the design).
